The Search Engine Feedback Loop

This simulator illustrates how search engines can contribute to the spread of misinformation through a feedback loop between users and algorithms. Research indicates that when people repeatedly click a particular result for a query, that behavior can contribute to the result receiving greater visibility for that query and related searches. Because the content people see first is also the content they will most likely click on, this new ranking position will continue to drive further engagement.
